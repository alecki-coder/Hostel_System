1. Eligibility. Only registered students may apply. Hostels Berlin, Moscow and Denver admit male students only. Hostels Nairobi and Tokyo admit female students only.
  2. One bed per student. A student may hold only one booking at a time. Each room holds a maximum of 4 students.
  3. Approval. A booking is a request until the warden approves it. The warden may reject any request, and may cancel an approved booking if these rules are broken.
  4. Room changes. Students may not swap rooms or move to another room without the warden's written permission.
  5. Visitors. Visitors are allowed only in the common areas, during the hours set by the warden, and must never stay overnight.
  6. Quiet hours. Noise must be kept to a minimum from 10:00 PM to 6:00 AM.
  7. Prohibited items. Weapons, illegal drugs, alcohol, cooking appliances and any item that is a fire hazard are not allowed in the hostel.
  8. Care of property. Students are responsible for the room and furniture assigned to them and must pay for any damage they cause.
  9. Truthful information. Giving false information when registering or booking leads to cancellation of the booking.
  10. Clearing. At the end of the stay, a student must be cleared by the warden. Clearing releases the bed immediately, so it can be given to another student.
